Privacy Policy

CoinLens · Last updated November 22, 2026

CoinLens is built so that your photos and your collection never leave your iPhone.

Data we collect

None. CoinLens does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK.

Camera and photos

With your permission, CoinLens uses the camera, or a photo you pick, to read the date and mint mark on a coin. Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Images are not stored by the app and are never transmitted.

Information stored on your device

Your saved coin collection, your silver price setting and a counter of free scans used are stored only on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Children

CoinLens does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
