Support

CoinLens · Last updated November 22, 2026

Need help with CoinLens? Email coinlens.app.usa@gmail.com.

The date was read wrong

On the result screen tap "Not right? Tap to correct" and type the year and mint mark. Corrections don't use a scan. Good, even light and filling the circle with the coin gives the best reads.

I don't have a photo of the coin

Tap "No photo? Type the date instead" on the scan screen.

Silver values look out of date

Open Settings and enter the current silver price per troy ounce. Every value updates immediately.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › CoinLens › Cancel Subscription.
