Estimates, not appraisals

Values shown in CoinLens are retail guide estimates for circulated coins, presented as ranges. They are not a professional grading, appraisal or offer to buy. Silver values depend on the silver price you set in the app. For a coin that may be valuable, use a professional grading service such as PCGS or NGC before buying or selling.

Free scans and purchases

Your first 3 scans are free. After that you can buy a pack of scan credits, a yearly CoinLens Pro subscription, or a one-time unlimited purchase. Prices are shown in the app before purchase.
